02

Colour

Contrast ratios shown below — toggle from the Tweaks panel

Why the floor sits above true black. A dim room adapts the eye's baseline down, so #000 next to white text and a critical-red chip creates simultaneous-contrast halation that gets worse across a 12-hour shift. The ground is lifted to roughly 9% luminance — dark enough that camera snapshots keep their own contrast, high enough that the eye is not fighting the interface itself by hour ten.

03

Type

Two families, deliberately not the defaults
Public Sans
UI and body face. Public Sans descends from Interstate — the actual US highway-signage letterform — so its DNA is already a road typeface tuned for legibility at speed and at small sizes, not a marketing display face pressed into service. Set at medium weight throughout; this system never bolds past 600.
0123456789
Tabular numeric face — timestamps, camera IDs, mile markers, confidence, live age counters. IBM Plex Mono was built for technical instrumentation, carries true tabular-lining figures, and reads like the OSD burned into a camera feed rather than a coder's terminal font.

Reconnecting pulse
1.6s loop · ease-in-out, opacity 1↔0.55. Runs only while degraded and stops the instant the state changes.
Reduced motion. Every duration above collapses to 0–100ms and drops its transform in favour of a plain opacity swap; anything that loops (only the reconnecting pulse does) stops looping and becomes a static state plus its label. No token here relies on motion to carry information that colour, icon and text don't already carry.
